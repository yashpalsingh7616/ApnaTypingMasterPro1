1. परिचय (Introduction)

Apna Typing Master Pro ("हम", "हमारा", "यह वेबसाइट") aapki privacy ka सम्मान karta hai। Ye policy batati hai ki hum aapki jaankari kaise collect, use, aur protect karte hain jab aap hamari website use karte hain।

2. Hum Kya Jaankari Collect Karte Hain

  • Account jaankari: Naam, email address, jab aap register karte hain

4. Jaankari Kisse Share Nahi Hoti

Hum aapki personal jaankari kisi third-party ko bechte ya kiraye par nahi dete। Sirf zaroori service providers (jaise Firebase — hosting/database, Razorpay — payment, Brevo — email) ke saath hi jaankari share hoti hai, sirf unka kaam poora karne ke liye।

5. Data Security

Aapka data Firebase (Google Cloud) par secure rehta hai। Passwords hash-encrypted hote hain — koi bhi, hum khud bhi, aapka asli password nahi dekh sakte। Payment details humare server se hokar guzarti hi nahi — seedhe Razorpay ke secure system mein jaati hain।

6. Aapke Adhikar

  • Apna data dekhne ya update karne ka adhikar
